## 3. Closed evidence grammar and bindings

These proposed **acceptance evidence** formats are not native APIs. Objects reject unknown/duplicate keys, floats, bool-as-int and trailing data. `ID` is 1..128 ASCII letters/digits or `_:.+-`; `SHA` is 40 lowercase hex; `Digest` is 64 lowercase hex; `Count` is an exact JSON integer 0..2147483647. All nanosecond fields use `NS`, a canonical decimal **string** (0 or nonzero digit followed by digits), parsed to an integer 0..18446744073709551615 without float conversion; overflow refuses. `UTC` is a generated timestamp in `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.ffffffZ`, validated Gregorian UTC, no leap-second representation. `NativeTime` is the exact original timezone-aware ISO-8601 string accepted by the pinned queue timestamp parser, with maximum 64 ASCII bytes. NativeAuthority retains those bytes unchanged; `approved_at_utc:UTC` is a separate derived parse normalized to UTC, never a replacement for the original. In particular `2026-10-14T15:57:38Z` remains those exact bytes and parses to `2026-10-14T15:57:38.000000Z`. Reject invalid, naive, overflowing or unrepresentable times; compare raw text to native readback and parsed instant to its derived field. `Artifact={id:ID,sha256:Digest}` resolves through the immutable private inventory; its named field supplies its role. No arbitrary role string selects authoritative evidence from generic evidence lists. For derived JSON identity digests only, canonical serialization is UTF-8 with lexicographically sorted object keys, no insignificant whitespace, ensure_ascii=true and no trailing newline; closed types admit no floats. Artifact and event-chain digests always hash the exact stored original bytes, not reserialization. Every referenced artifact is hash-verified; lists named evidence are supplementary only and never replace a mandatory field.

## 7. One exact same-boot segment and autonomous observation

The segment starts only after valid Entry and all prerequisites. `duration_ns` is **exclusively the monotonic endpoint delta**, parsed as exact NS integers: final checkpoint.monotonic_ns minus segment_start.monotonic_ns. Qualification requires >=259200000000000 ns (72 hours) without rounding or adding intervals. The admitted source is CLOCK_BOOTTIME, including suspension, fixed for the segment. All endpoints share boot_id and clock source; a boot change interrupts clock_uncertain, even after successful automatic recovery. UTC remains independent chronology/cross-check: pair UTC and monotonic samples in one bounded capture, never decrease, and require absolute UTC/monotonic elapsed discrepancy <=1000000000 ns for consecutive full checkpoints and the full segment. UTC does not determine duration. A monotonic 259200.2s/UTC259199.4s interval qualifies on duration if all other invariants pass; 259199999999999 monotonic ns never qualifies.

**Proposed collector, not installed capability.** acceptance_integration must supply a reviewed read-only collector/evaluator and bounded source-specific adapters. `Checkpoint={id:ID,entry_sha256:Digest,boot_id:ID,clock_source:"boottime",at:UTC,monotonic_ns:NS,identity:Identity,inventory_observation:Artifact,audit_intervals:[AuditInterval],source_cursors:Artifact,continuity:Artifact,result:"pass"|"hold"|"fail"}`. `AuditInterval={source_id:ID,from_cursor:ID,to_cursor:ID,from_monotonic_ns:NS,to_monotonic_ns:NS,evidence:Artifact}`.
