Internal mode preserves current recurring loops and their fatal-task handling and refuses external scheduled ingress. Systemd mode omits only internal recurring pickup/reconcile loops; startup recovery, event pickup, manual control and quota one-shots remain.

## 4. Idempotent wake ingress, origin and bounds

Propose `wsd scheduled-wake pickup|reconcile` as a narrow client over the existing private control socket, plus a strict corresponding request variant. Decode raw validated JSON into a **separate strict ScheduledWakeRequest struct**, never widen or add UUIDs to the legacy CtlRequest.generation:int|None. Distinct EffectWakeRequest and PairedBackupRequest structs likewise own their UUID generation fields; legacy resolve/sandbox-settle retain their exact integer generation contract. Exact fields: `op="scheduled-wake"`, `version=1`, lowercase UUID strings `installation_id`, `generation`, `request_id`, and `job` in `{pickup,reconcile}`. Unknown/duplicate/missing keys, wrong types and noncanonical UUIDs refuse. No targets or occurrence digest come from the caller; WSD selects the exact installed allowlist. Retain the shared legacy framing limit exactly: **4096 request payload bytes excluding the trailing newline**, reader limit `MAX_REQUEST+2`, reply frame at most1 MiB including newline. Additionally cap a valid new wake payload at1024 bytes excluding newline. Do not tighten legacy requests or raise shared limits.

Do not move listener startup earlier. Missing/refused connection during startup is `wake_unconfirmed`, with no claim of enqueue; the health snapshot separately shows in-progress recovery. Once listening, WSD validates owner, loaded manifest and readiness (not maintenance/stopping) before enqueue. Pickup calls `_backstop` through a **distinct scheduled lane key** `("scheduled-pickup", None)`, never `_operator_pickup` or the manual/event pickup key. Reconcile uses `("scheduled-reconcile", None)` and `reconcile_one`. Both use the existing job-error wrapper, per-workstream operation locks and quota rearming. Duplicate wakes join only queued scheduled work. A later wake while one runs can queue one further job per key; backlog is bounded at one running and one queued scheduled job of each kind per lane. No job starts after the existing shutdown boundary.

This is at-least-once wake delivery. It is safe to issue a later wake after timeout because scheduler/recovery effect guards, not wake exactly-once, govern actions. There is **no durable wake intent, not-issued proof, tick-status settlement or uncertain wake ledger**. Losing a wake during process failure is covered by startup and the next timer. A wake response is not proof that a native write, review, launch, send or task completed.

Origin association is diagnostic and bounded **in memory only**: no extra per-core-wake journal rows, fsynced history or replay keys. Extend the proposed scheduled submission primitive so `Lane.lock` covers lookup/join, count, first/last request IDs, up to64 IDs and `origins_truncated`, and the worker’s dequeue freezes that association under the same lock. Never mutate provenance after `submit` returns. Manual and scheduled origins remain distinct. Keep only queued/running and one latest completed summary per workstream/kind in the cached health projection; restart loses it, explicitly. Existing task/action journal effects retain their native identities and retention; this change does not invent whole-journal retention or claim historical per-request completeness.

Preserve the legacy `CtlReply(result,message,data)` frame for **every** request, including new ones. Proposed server decoding adds a strict discriminated scheduled/effect/backup variant after bounded framing and raw-JSON key validation; existing op decoding/refusal stays byte-compatible. The new handler’s `data["scheduled"]` is a dictionary of strings exactly: `version="1"`, echoed UUID `request_id`, `status` in `{queued,refused}`, `reason` in `{none,owner_mismatch,generation_mismatch,manifest_mismatch,maintenance,stopping,unsupported_job}`, decimal `targets`0–128 and `joined`0–targets. `queued` requires legacy result `ok` and reason none; typed pre-enqueue refusal requires legacy result `refused`, targets/joined0. Malformed/oversized/read-timeout/unrecognized input, closing-before-dispatch and unexpected exceptions keep the current legacy `refused`/`failed` envelope with empty data. No typed invalid-ID/null response is promised. The new client accepts these generic envelopes as unconfirmed/not admitted by this response, never guesses a stable reason from prose or definite-unsent effects. A queued response means all target submissions admitted in one event-loop turn without await, not completed jobs. Precheck all lanes before submission; unexpected partial submit is fatal: close new admission, signal the serving task to enter its existing bounded drain and raise/restart path, no permanent healthy-but-closed mode. Already admitted work follows native drain/recovery; no success reply or fake rollback. Unit restart policy stays the existing reviewed service contract.

Every admitted scheduled future has one result consumer installed **before** returning queued. Its done callback uses the serving loop thread-safe handoff to retrieve the result exactly once; JobFailed means the existing _guarded successfully recorded the ordinary job failure, and Stopping/cancellation during shutdown is expected. Every other exception, including failed tick_failed journal writes, sets the first-exception fatal latch watched by serve together with stop/timers. serve closes admission and executes the same bounded drain/finally path, then raises the original exception. Joiners attach no duplicate fatal consumers. Callback failure itself also trips the latch. No unobserved future or queued-reply success suppresses a fatal exception. This preserves the daemon.py:634–703 rule for external wakes and shares the partial-submit fatal path.

The oneshot has a five-second total monotonic connect/write/read deadline and4096-byte complete legacy response-frame cap. Invalid/oversized/timeout response records only `wake_unconfirmed`; there is no blind claim of success or irreversible uncertainty hold. One request per job per activation, no immediate retry loop; the next timer retries naturally. Systemd `TimeoutStartSec=10s`, no automatic oneshot `Restart=` loop. This prevents old uncertainty work from starving fresh pickup. Workstreams remain independent after admission.

## 5. Effect scheduling without a second authority

Only effectful jobs need persistent deduplication. New private schedule-store schema1 is **owned solely by the WSD process**, through a dedicated effect-state writer lane and RLock-protected connection; the CLI runner never opens or writes it. Babysitter has a separate observation store and no access to this writer. Reuse journal infrastructure helpers for private directories, SQLite online backup and transaction/busy semantics while retaining a distinct strict versioned schema (do not construct a mutating Journal over a different schema). Missing after prior installation, corrupt, unexpected schema or failed upgrade refuses effect admission and reports a hold; it never blocks idempotent core wakes merely because an effect history is missing.

Proposed typed `effect-wake` (optional producer jobs only, **not backup_bundle**) is a separate request variant with the same legacy envelope and4096-byte payload socket, carrying version/install/generation/request UUIDs and registered effect `job` only. It returns bounded enqueue/refusal, not effect success. WSD computes eligibility and exact slot from its installed registry and clock; the caller supplies neither opaque keys nor invented slots. At most one queued/running evaluation per effect job, round-robin among enabled jobs. No store lock is held across a producer call. The sole writer lane publishes an immutable cached aggregate after each committed transition and once after store recovery; it publishes an explicit unready/error aggregate if recovery fails. The event loop consumes only that cache, never SQLite or the store RLock. One pass has30 seconds, at most8 producer calls, each at most5 seconds subject to remaining total budget; reserve at least one call for a newly due job if any, then alternate unresolved reconciliation and new due jobs. Producer calls with a larger required budget cannot activate under this contract. Per-effect unknown can hold that job but cannot starve other jobs or wakes.

Stable effect identity is canonical JSON array `[1, installation_id, generation, job, slot]`, hashed with SHA256 by the authority. `job` is the registry ID and schedule ID; there is no separate ambiguous namespace. `slot` is the **local civil date ordinal** in the installed IANA timezone for calendar daily jobs (UTC jobs use UTC civil date). Calendar resolution pins intended civil date/time, resolved UTC instant, timezone and chosen offset alongside it. Changing timezone/time changes generation; never derive local-day identity from UTC day. One configured civil occurrence per day: repeated local time chooses first offset; a nonexistent time advances to the first valid instant. Calendar sequence must strictly increase; backward UTC never reopens a consumed slot. Each generation pins an activation UTC instant. For every calendar job reject an occurrence whose resolved UTC due instant precedes that activation, including today's earlier occurrence; at exact equality admit once. This forbids reinstall/timezone changes from replaying an already-due day under a new generation. No historical catch-up before activation is promised. Unsettled prior-generation effects still block that job as §10 requires. Interval jobs retain the same pre-generation rejection rule. For future interval effects the immutable registry defines integer UTC anchor A/period P and `slot=floor((now-A)/P)`; reject negative/pre-generation-start slots. Any such adapter must first be admitted with its exact producer contract. Coalesce missed *unissued* slots to latest eligible; record aggregate skipped count, never replay each.

Per `(installation_id,generation,job)` keep O(1) `highest_consumed_slot`, `skipped_count`, latest terminal receipt summary, and at most one unsettled occurrence. Write intent and dispatch mark before calling producer. States: `prepared` (no dispatch mark; safely resumable), `dispatched`, `succeeded`, `definite_unsent`, `uncertain`, `held`. A committed dispatch mark without a compatible authoritative receipt is uncertain, including after restart. Later slots of that job wait until its actual producer settles it. No absence-at-a-point-in-time claim proves an old in-flight request unsent. Producer settlement must fence late issue, or affirmatively prove effect outcome; without that support, retain uncertain and request genuine Marmot recovery consent, never replay. Other jobs continue.

A success receipt means its exact adapter-defined effect (durable producer enqueue; backup has its separate cycle identity in §8); actual remote delivery/operator acceptance remains separate. Definite-unsent may retry the same slot only if the producer proves it cannot arrive later. Advancing high-water and storing terminal result is one store transaction. High-water covers all old slots even after recent detail compacts. Keep recent terminal receipts up to30 days and at most4096 rows globally, evicting oldest terminal detail only; no dedup dependency on these rows. At most8 enabled effect jobs and8 unsettled rows per active generation; retain unresolved old generations (at most16 total generations with unsettled effects, then refuse only new effect-generation activation). No 100k lifetime limit or routine rotation of core scheduling. Full raw history belongs to producer audit/retention policy; count/hash compaction is disclosed, not fictitious complete history.

Effect clocks start unready on each process/boot. Proposed readiness envelope: installation/generation, boot ID, clock epoch, UTC and monotonic samples, durable highest admitted UTC and typed reason `{ready,recovering,unsynchronized,rollback,jump,invalid_config}`. Require store recovery, valid timezone, affirmative platform synchronization evidence, and two samples at least30 monotonic seconds apart with elapsed discrepancy at most2 seconds; refuse calendar admission behind durable UTC high-water. Rollback/jump or synchronization loss clears readiness; monotonic samples never span boots. This gate applies only effect due evaluation. Pickup, reconcile, observation and ordinary deterministic commands continue. Plan6C is not presumed to implement this proposed envelope.
