[BLOCKING] `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-au11-admind-accounts-design.md:51` — An empty default overlay preserves inherited `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` or `CODEX_HOME`, while the recorded key identifies AU-2’s fixed `~/.claude` or `~/.codex`.
