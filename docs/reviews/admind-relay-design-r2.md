Verdict: REVISE

1. **[BLOCKING] [plan:519](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay.md:519): Whole-text redaction equality does not guarantee that delivered chunks remain unchanged.** `Admind.post` redacts each chunk again ([daemon.py:415](src/heterodyne/admind/daemon.py:415)). Splitting can introduce a boundary that makes a previously unmatched secret pattern match. A read-only reproduction against the actual redactor and splitter returned `whole_survives=True`, `every_chunk_survives=False`. Thus R21 can admit content that is subsequently hidden while every chunk counts as delivered. **Fix:** validate the exact final card and details chunks against the redaction performed by `post`, before enqueueing anything. Refuse if any chunk changes. Add a boundary-aligned regression test for both card and details delivery.

2. **[MAJOR] [spec:284](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay-design.md:284), [plan:529](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay.md:529): The current attempt’s lifecycle remains undefined.** The spec records an outcome in `settled`, including `uncertain`, but `attempt_for` returns “the unsettled one.” The design does not say how an uncertain attempt remains retrievable. Conversely, attempts inserted before preflight refusals can remain unsettled after the ask returns to `open`; a subsequent decision can then produce multiple candidates. Reconciliation also lacks an explicit atomic update of the attempt and ask. **Fix:** define a persisted current-attempt identity and precise terminal versus unresolved outcomes. Close every prelaunch refusal atomically, retain uncertain attempts for retry, and atomically settle the attempt, ask and notice. Test refusal→retry→restart and uncertain→successful reconciliation.

3. **[MAJOR] [plan:548](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay.md:548): Startup reconciliation can select a live decision.** The planned task starts after `may_post`, when the normal worker can already process commands, and scans `deciding` asks without specifying a startup snapshot or serialization. A new decision persists `deciding` before its first awaited read. Reconciliation can read the still-undecided bead and reopen that ask while its decision is about to launch. The bead lock cannot prevent this: it protects individual child invocations, not the parent’s entire decision flow. **Fix:** capture recovery candidates before starting workers and restrict startup reconciliation to those exact attempts. Specify serialization and conditional state updates so reconciliation cannot reopen an active attempt. Test reconciliation while a live decision is paused after attempt insertion.

4. **[MAJOR] [plan:269](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay.md:269), [plan:346](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay.md:346): Blocked asks permanently consume the global admission quota.** `blocked` counts toward the 20 non-terminal asks, but cancellation accepts only `open`/`answered`, reconciliation excludes `blocked`, and the spec declares it final for the relay. Resolving the bead at the terminal therefore releases no capacity. Twenty accumulated blocked asks disable posting questions, merges and approvals indefinitely. **Fix:** exclude final blocked records from the admission count, while retaining their same-bead decision prohibition, or provide an explicit archival transition that releases capacity without permitting another decision. Test posting after blocked outcomes have exhausted the current quota.

5. **[MINOR] [plan:118](docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay.md:118), [spec:190](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-admind-marmot-relay-design.md:190): Captured gate stderr does not always contain a rejection reason.** In the beads-task-queue checkout, `bin/btq:593`–`599` rejects several conditions silently, including approver membership, ADR revision, review validity and the required pin. Consequently, `gate_valid=false` can accompany an empty `gate_reasons`, although the prescribed reply requires its first element. **Fix:** provide a safe fixed fallback for empty reasons, or make every rejection predicate emit a reason and explicitly include that BTQ change in the plan. Test a real gate rejection with empty stderr.
