[BLOCKING] `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-au4-deferral-design.md:157` — **Putting the startup re-gate in recovery introduces a timer re-gate.** `Wsd.reconcile_one()` invokes recovery periodically, and failed jobs also cause recovery before later pickups. Consequently, “recovery’s last step” can re-gate `account_changed` without startup, reload or release, violating D5. **Fix:** distinguish process-start re-gating from ordinary recovery. Exercise the daemon’s periodic reconcile and recovery-retry paths in the no-timer test; calling only `Scheduler.pickup()` would miss this defect.

[BLOCKING] `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-au4-deferral-design.md:54` — **The replay dispatch change does not cover PICKUP ops containing a defer tail.** The spec changes `Parker.replay`, but `Scheduler.replay` routes PICKUP directly through `_start`, which currently falls through to `Parker.launch` except for the legacy `quota` step. After a guard-defer crash, replay could launch or allocate another deferral instead of completing the recorded one. An undefer that re-defers also carries both discriminator keys. **Fix:** define dispatch for all three containing kinds, with an active defer tail taking precedence over undefer and launch handling. **O4: acceptable in principle**, provided these routes and their crash tests are explicit; a schema change is unnecessary.
