[BLOCKING] **Redact the full title before shortening it.** [B4, spec:14](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-admind-asks-bump-design.md:14) specifies the first 80 title characters, followed by redaction. This can expose a secret fragment: the recognizer requires at least 64 hex characters ([redact.py:26](src/heterodyne/admind/redact.py:26)). A read-only reproduction showed a 97-character title being safely redacted whole, but exposing 47 secret characters after truncation. **Fix:** require `redact(row.title)[:80]`, then redact the complete notice before splitting. Add a test with a secret crossing character 80.

2. [BLOCKING] **B1 and B6 need explicit routing precedence.** [B1, spec:11](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-admind-asks-bump-design.md:11) permits `!asks bump` as any reply; B6 requires every reply to a bump to receive only a hint. Today, plain replies to `asknote:` targets reach `held` and the agent ([daemon.py:1757](src/heterodyne/admind/daemon.py:1757)); reactions are ignored ([daemon.py:1721](src/heterodyne/admind/daemon.py:1721)). **Fix:** explicitly preserve independent commands such as `!asks bump` and ID-addressed `!answer`; intercept plain replies and card-decision commands on bumps. Use a separate, exact, sent-outbox bump recognizer after existing ingress checks. Keep `_CARD_KEY` and `ask_for_message` unchanged. This makes B6 implementable without granting reminders card authority.

3. [BLOCKING] **The test plan misses important threading and security boundaries.** [Spec:26](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-admind-asks-bump-design.md:26) covers repeated bumps, multiple card chunks and details, but omits R29 refreshes. B3’s anchor is otherwise correct: `first_card_message` selects exactly `ask:<id>:0` ([store.py:651](src/heterodyne/admind/store.py:651)). **Fix:** add tests proving a refreshed ask’s bumps target its own new chunk 0, while its stale predecessor and undecidable updated-content chunks are excluded. Also test bump-reply/reaction replay, latch and removed-operator rejection, authorisation loss during reconcile, exact sent-only bump recognition, and ordinary non-bump notices retaining their routing. Assert no answer, decision attempt, subprocess or agent paste.

4. [NON-BLOCKING] **`open` and `answered` are appropriate, but “until their originator collects them” is inaccurate.** [B2, spec:12](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-admind-asks-bump-design.md:12) correctly excludes `deciding` and `uncertain` from actionable reminders; all four count toward `ASK_ACTIVE` ([store.py:118](src/heterodyne/admind/store.py:118)). However, `get` does not change status ([daemon.py:635](src/heterodyne/admind/daemon.py:635)), and `wait` only reads it. **Fix:** say answered asks remain active until explicitly cancelled. Describe their reminder as “answered; awaiting closure” rather than inviting another answer, and test that collecting an answer does not remove it from bump eligibility.

5. [NON-BLOCKING] **B8’s message bound conflicts with chunking.** [Spec:18](docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-admind-asks-bump-design.md:18) promises at most `MAX_OPEN` messages plus one summary, but `chunk_chars` may be 200 ([settings.py:109](src/heterodyne/admind/settings.py:109)). An ordinary approval reminder with an 80-character title is already 205 characters; summaries can also split. **Fix:** promise at most `MAX_OPEN` logical reminders plus a logical summary, and express the transport bound using their actual chunk counts. Test minimum-size chunking and ensure every reminder chunk targets the original card.
